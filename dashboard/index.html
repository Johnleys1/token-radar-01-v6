Size Curve Radar

Implied value is this radar's own claim: what a protocol's token would be worth if its record stayed as it is. It is not a prediction that a token will launch, or when.

Why the curve, not a flat multiple

A flat multiple forces log(mcap) to grow 1-for-1 with log(TVL). The fitted slope is far from 1, so big protocols carry lower multiples and small ones higher. The curve is kept because it beats the flat multiple out of sample — on both blind tests below. Absolute hit rates are low for every model here (TVL alone explains only part of value); what matters is the edge over the baseline, shown as a multiplier.

Method: pooled OLS log(mcap) = a + b·log(TVL) on the token protocols in the sealed snapshot; every extra term tested held-out twice and dropped unless it wins both tests. Farm plans are costed for a $5,000 deposit over 30 days: bridge + gas + 0.30% DEX fee + slippage + exit, against an expected farmer reward of 5% of implied value shared pro-rata by TVL. Rank range: lowest–highest rank across 200 bootstrap refits of the curve. Coverage: every watchlist protocol is on the board; all 758 snapshot protocols are accounted for (live anchors on the second tab).
